04 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to unblock a drain?

The price is fixed for any type of problem and already includes the call-out, so the price we quote is the final price. We don't publish it on the website: call us and we'll tell you the price straight away.

What is a CCTV inspection?

It is not video surveillance. It is a small camera fitted to the end of the hose that goes into the pipe and shows what is inside: roots, grease build-up, broken or badly connected pipes. It also lets us map the sewer layout and find every inspection chamber. We use it when needed.

Do you work with condominiums and businesses?

Yes. We work with homeowners, condominiums, restaurants, hotels and businesses across the Algarve.

Do I need to be there during the job?

We arrange everything by phone. What matters is that we can get to the inspection chambers and the problem areas.
